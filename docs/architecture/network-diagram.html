💡 How to use the Explorer

This interactive canvas models the physical and logical architecture of Joel's home network.

  • Click on michael, KeepLINK Switch, or Zita to inspect interfaces and firewall rules.
  • Use the view buttons in the top header to isolate Physical Ports, VLANs, or Firewall Flows.
  • Drag the diagram canvas to pan, and use the zoom buttons or mouse scroll to zoom.
